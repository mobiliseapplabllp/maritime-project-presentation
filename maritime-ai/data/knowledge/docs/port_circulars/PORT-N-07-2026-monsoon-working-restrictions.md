# Port Notice PORT-N-07/2026 — Monsoon working restrictions: West Basin and outer anchorage

Issued by: Harbour Master, Mundra (Capt. Rajiv Nair) · Issued: 15 June 2026 · Effective: 25 June 2026
Acknowledgement required: YES (all shipping agents and terminal operators)
Tags: monsoon, UKC, bunkering, West Basin, anchorage

Register summary (verbatim): "Cape vessels to maintain 20% additional UKC at WB
berths during SW monsoon; bunkering suspended at outer anchorage above sea
state 5."

## Text of the notice (as issued)

"During the South-West monsoon period, all capesize vessels berthing at
WB-1/WB-2 shall maintain an additional 20% under-keel clearance over the
declared minimum. Bunker barge operations at the outer anchorage stand
suspended whenever sea state exceeds 5 or wind exceeds 25 knots sustained.
Masters shall confirm compliance on the pre-arrival checklist."

## 1. Applicability and duration

1.1 This notice applies within Mundra port limits for the declared South-West
monsoon period. The Harbour Master declares the opening and closing of the
monsoon period by message to all agents; it ordinarily runs from June to the
end of September.

1.2 The under-keel clearance (UKC) provision applies to capesize dry bulk
vessels working the West Basin berths WB-1 and WB-2 (West Basin Coal Terminal).
The bunkering provision applies to all bunker barge operations at the outer
anchorage, irrespective of vessel type.

## 2. Under-keel clearance at WB-1 / WB-2

2.1 The declared minimum UKC for the West Basin remains as promulgated in the
port's standing navigational data. During the monsoon period, capesize vessels
shall plan arrival drafts, tidal windows and ballast conditions so that the UKC
actually available alongside and in the approach is not less than 120% of that
declared minimum (that is, the declared minimum plus a further 20%).

2.2 Pilots will verify the computed UKC on the master–pilot exchange. Where the
20% additional margin cannot be demonstrated for the intended tidal window, the
movement will be rescheduled to a suitable tide or the vessel will be lightened
at anchorage as arranged by the agent.

2.3 Swell allowance: the additional margin accounts for monsoon swell response
(heave, pitch and squat at low speed). It is not to be traded against squat
calculations; the 20% is applied after squat and swell response are deducted.

## 3. Bunkering at the outer anchorage

3.1 Bunker barge operations at the outer anchorage stand suspended whenever
either of the following is observed or forecast for the operation window:
(a) sea state exceeding 5 on the WMO scale; or
(b) sustained wind exceeding 25 knots.

3.2 Marine Control is the deciding authority on prevailing conditions and will
broadcast suspensions and resumptions on the port working channel (VHF Ch 12).
Barges already alongside a receiving vessel when a suspension is declared shall
stop transfer, drain and disconnect hoses, and cast off in a controlled manner.

3.3 Licensed bunker suppliers (see the port licence register — e.g. Saurashtra
Bunkers LLP) shall plan deliveries against the marine forecast and shall not
push a delivery into a closing weather window. See also Mundra SOP MP-02
(bunkering safety checklist).

## 4. Mooring and berth safety during the monsoon

4.1 The pre-monsoon mooring standard for cape vessels at the West Basin is
raised for the duration of this notice: masters shall run additional lines as
directed by the pilot or berthing master, and shall keep winch brakes tested
and rendered to the correct settings.

4.2 A tug may be held on standby at the Harbour Master's discretion during
squall passage. Costs follow the schedule of marine charges.

## 5. Pre-arrival confirmation

5.1 Masters shall confirm compliance with this notice on the pre-arrival
checklist submitted through the agent with the vessel's pre-arrival
notification. Calls that cannot confirm the UKC margin will be scheduled
accordingly.

## 6. Supersession

6.1 For the monsoon months, this notice supersedes Circular CIRC-04/2025
("Anchorage allocation policy — priority matrix", issued by the Harbour Master
in July 2025). The register entry for that circular now reads, verbatim:
"Superseded by PORT-N-07/2026 for monsoon months." Outside the declared monsoon
period, anchorage allocation continues on the priority-matrix principles
(laden tankers on demurrage, then liner-schedule container vessels, then bulk
carriers by ETA order, with bunker-only calls taking the outer stations), until
a consolidated anchorage policy is reissued.
