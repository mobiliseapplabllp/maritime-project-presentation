# Circular CIRC-12/2026 — Electronic port clearance: mandatory FAL declarations via portal

> Demo-world instrument of the Mundra Port Operations Portal, reproduced in the
> Sagar Drishti knowledge library. This is a fictional demo document and not a
> publication of any real port authority.

Ref No: CIRC-12/2026 · Type: CIRCULAR · Category: Port operations · Status: IN FORCE
Issued by: Port Operations, Mundra · Issued: 15 July 2026 · Effective: 14 August 2026
Acknowledgement required: YES (all shipping agents)
Tags: FAL, single window, pre-arrival, electronic clearance

Register summary (verbatim): "All agents to submit FAL 1–7 declarations
electronically; paper submissions cease from effective date."

## Text of the circular (as issued)

"Pursuant to the FAL Convention (2022 amendments), all shipping agents shall
lodge General Declaration, Cargo Declaration, Ship Stores, Crew Effects, Crew
List, Passenger List and Dangerous Goods declarations through the port
community interface. Paper declarations will not be accepted after the
effective date except for declared system outages."

## 1. Background

1.1 The IMO Convention on Facilitation of International Maritime Traffic (FAL),
as amended in 2022, obliges public authorities to exchange arrival, stay and
departure information electronically through a maritime single window. This
circular brings the port's clearance workflow fully onto the port community
interface and retires the interim paper/email format.

1.2 A reference summary of the FAL Convention and its standard declarations is
held in the Sagar Drishti library ("IMO FAL Convention — declarations
overview").

## 2. Declarations covered

The following shall be lodged electronically for every arrival and departure,
using the portal's structured forms (which mirror the IMO FAL forms):

| Portal form | IMO FAL form | Content |
|---|---|---|
| General Declaration | FAL Form 1 | Ship particulars, voyage, master, agent |
| Cargo Declaration | FAL Form 2 | Cargo on board / to be worked |
| Ship's Stores Declaration | FAL Form 3 | Stores, bunkers ROB, slop and sludge figures |
| Crew's Effects Declaration | FAL Form 4 | Dutiable crew effects |
| Crew List | FAL Form 5 | Crew with rank, nationality, documents |
| Passenger List | FAL Form 6 | Passengers (where carried) |
| Dangerous Goods | FAL Form 7 | DG manifest with IMDG class, UN No., stowage |

## 3. Submission windows

3.1 Pre-arrival: General Declaration, Crew List, Dangerous Goods and the
pre-arrival checklist (including confirmation of compliance with Port Notice
PORT-N-07/2026 during the monsoon) not later than 48 hours before ETA, or on
departure from the last port where the passage is shorter.

3.2 Cargo Declaration: before commencement of cargo work. Ship's Stores and
Crew's Effects: on arrival. Departure set: before the outward pilot is booked.

3.3 Amendments are lodged as portal revisions; the latest accepted revision
governs. The portal timestamps every submission for clearance audit.

## 4. Paper cut-over and outage fallback

4.1 From the effective date, paper and email declarations are not accepted.
The sole exception is a system outage declared by Port Operations on the portal
status page and by broadcast to agents; during a declared outage the interim
format under the former Circular CIRC-03/2023 may be used, and the data shall
be back-entered within 24 hours of restoration.

## 5. Supersession history

5.1 This circular supersedes Circular CIRC-03/2023 ("Pre-arrival notification
format revised — IMO FAL forms", issued 4 September 2023), which had introduced
an interim paper/email FAL format. The register entry for CIRC-03/2023 now
reads, verbatim: "Interim paper/email FAL format ahead of the portal
single-window rollout. Superseded by CIRC-12/2026."

## 6. Compliance

6.1 Vessels whose declaration set is incomplete will not be granted berth
allocation priority, and outward clearance will be held until the departure set
is accepted. Repeated non-compliance by an agent is a licence-condition matter
under the port's agency licensing scheme.
