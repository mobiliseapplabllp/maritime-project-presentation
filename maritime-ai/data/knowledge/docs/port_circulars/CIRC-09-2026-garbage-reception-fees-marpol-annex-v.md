## 1. Purpose

1.1 This circular revises the charges for the port's garbage reception service
and tightens the documentation required for larger landings, so that reception
remains adequate under MARPOL Annex V (garbage from ships) without encouraging
mixed, unsegregated landings.

1.2 A reference summary of MARPOL and its six annexes is held in the Sagar
Drishti library ("MARPOL 73/78 — Annexes I–VI overview").

## 2. Service and booking

2.1 Garbage reception is booked through the vessel's agent on the port
community interface at least 12 hours before the intended landing. Collection
is by licensed contractor barge/truck at the berth; anchorage collections run
subject to weather (see Port Notice PORT-N-07/2026 during the monsoon).

2.2 Vessels shall present garbage segregated by MARPOL Annex V category
(plastics; food wastes; domestic wastes; cooking oil; incinerator ashes;
operational wastes; e-waste; cargo residues). Plastics are never landed mixed
with food waste.

## 3. Revised charges (demo tariff)

| Item | Basis | Revised charge |
|---|---|---|
| Standard garbage call (up to 2 m³, segregated) | per call | Rs 9,500 |
| Additional volume beyond 2 m³ | per m³ | Rs 3,200 |
| Unsegregated landing surcharge | per call | 50% of the bill |
| Cooking oil / operational waste in drums | per drum | Rs 750 |
| Cancellation within 6 hours of slot | per booking | Rs 2,500 |

3.1 Charges are billed to the agent with the port call invoice under the
garbage-reception head of the schedule of port tariffs.

## 4. Segregation certificate for landings above 2 m³

4.1 Vessels landing more than 2 m³ in one call shall lodge a segregation
certificate signed by the master or chief officer, stating the volume by
category and confirming that no plastics are mixed with other streams. The
contractor may refuse an uncertified or visibly mixed landing.

4.2 The landing receipt issued by the contractor is to be retained with the
vessel's Garbage Record Book entry for the landing, consistent with MARPOL
Annex V record-keeping.

## 5. Compliance notes

5.1 Discharge of garbage into port waters is prohibited. Observed floating
debris or overflow from skips is reported and handled as an environment
incident through the port incident-management workflow.

5.2 The port publishes any alleged inadequacy of reception facilities to the
flag and to the Directorate General of Shipping as required; masters should
report inadequacies via the agent in the IMO standard format.
