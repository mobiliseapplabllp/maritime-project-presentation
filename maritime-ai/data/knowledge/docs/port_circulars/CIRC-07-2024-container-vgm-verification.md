# Circular CIRC-07/2024 — Container VGM verification procedure at the gate

Register summary (verbatim): "Verified Gross Mass to be declared and weighed at
the terminal gate for all export containers per SOLAS Ch VI reg 2."

## Text of the circular (as issued)

"Shipping agents and CHAs shall submit VGM via Method 1 (weighing the packed
container) at the terminal gate weighbridge; Method 2 (calculated) declarations
must carry the shipper's certified weighing procedure on file. Containers
without a VGM record on file will not be permitted to load."

## 1. Background

1.1 SOLAS Chapter VI, regulation 2 requires the shipper to provide the Verified
Gross Mass (VGM) of a packed export container before it may be loaded on board.
Two methods are recognised internationally: Method 1 — weighing the packed and
sealed container; Method 2 — weighing all packages, dunnage and securing
material and adding the container tare, under a certified procedure.

1.2 This circular standardises how VGM is captured at the container terminals
(MICT, AMCT, AMC2, CT-3, CT-4) so that the planning systems hold a single
authoritative VGM per container.

## 2. Procedure at the gate

2.1 Method 1 (default): every export container passes the terminal gate
weighbridge on entry. The weighbridge reading, less the certified tare of the
road vehicle, is recorded as the VGM against the container number and shipper,
and flows automatically to the terminal operating system and the load planner.

2.2 Method 2 (by exception): a shipper approved for Method 2 lodges the
calculated VGM in the pre-advice. The approval requires the shipper's certified
weighing procedure to be on file with the terminal and referenced in each
declaration. Method 2 boxes may still be check-weighed at the gate.

2.3 Discrepancy handling: where the gate weight differs from a declared VGM by
more than the permitted tolerance of ±5% or 500 kg (whichever is less), the
gate weight prevails, the pre-advice is flagged for the shipper's account, and
repeated discrepancies are reported to the line and to the licensing desk.

## 3. No VGM — no load

3.1 A container without an accepted VGM record on file will not be permitted to
load. It will be held in a designated yard block at the shipper's cost until
the VGM is established by weighing.

3.2 Terminal planners shall not accept manual overrides of the VGM field;
corrections flow only through a revised weighment or a revised Method 2
declaration.

## 4. Records and audit

4.1 Weighbridge calibration certificates are maintained by each terminal and
are auditable by the port. VGM records are retained for the period specified in
the terminal's document-retention schedule and are produced on request for
flag, PSC or casualty inquiries.
